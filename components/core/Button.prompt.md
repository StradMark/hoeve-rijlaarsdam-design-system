One-line: the pill CTA used everywhere on Hoeve Rijlaarsdam — uppercase Playfair, wide letterspacing, fully rounded, never a square button.

```jsx
<Button tone="primary" size="md">Vraag offerte aan</Button>
<Button tone="gold" size="md">Versturen</Button>
<Button tone="outlineLight" size="sm">Catering</Button>
```

Tones: `primary` deep green (page CTAs, nav "Offerte"), `gold` (form submit only), `outlineLight` (over photography or the sage band), `outlineDark` (cream sections), `white` (inside a deep-green panel). Hover darkens the fill or inverts the outline; press shrinks 3%.
