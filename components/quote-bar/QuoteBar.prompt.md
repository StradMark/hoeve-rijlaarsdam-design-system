```jsx
<QuoteBar>&ldquo;Daar waar hospitality een kunstvorm is!&rdquo;</QuoteBar>
```

One line only, and short — it is set in capitals, so anything over ~70 characters wraps and loses its authority. Keep the typographic quotes. Never italic (that was an early mistake) and never body sans. It always spans the full page width with no container, and only ever appears directly below the hero. Deep green only — never on sage: cream capitals on `--surface-sage` measure about 2.2:1 and fail contrast.
