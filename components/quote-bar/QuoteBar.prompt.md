One-line: the deep-green band under the hero carrying one short line about that page — display serif in the case it is written in, never capitals.

```jsx
<QuoteBar>&ldquo;Daar waar hospitality een kunstvorm is!&rdquo;</QuoteBar>
```

One line only, and short — anything over ~70 characters wraps and loses its authority. **Never capitals**: op verzoek van de ontwerper (“kapitalen is voor mij schreeuwen”) staat een quote in kleine letters; caps zijn voorbehouden aan RIJLAARSDAM in het logo en aan labels. Keep the typographic quotes. Never italic (that was an early mistake) and never body sans. It always spans the full page width with no container, and only ever appears directly below the hero. Deep green only — never on sage: cream text on `--surface-sage` measures about 2.2:1 and fails contrast.
